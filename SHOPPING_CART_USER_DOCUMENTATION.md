# Shopping Cart Feature - Customer-Facing Overview

## What's New: Shopping Cart Feature 🛒

You can now add membership plans to your shopping cart and purchase multiple items at once. Here's what you can do:

---

## How to Use the Cart

### 1️⃣ Add Plans to Your Cart
- Go to the **Plans** page
- Browse available membership plans
- Click the **"Add to Cart"** button on any plan
- You can add the same plan multiple times or add different plans
- **Pro Tip:** Add multiple plans to compare before purchasing

### 2️⃣ See Your Items Count
- Look at the header (top-right corner)
- The **Shopping Cart Icon** now displays a **red badge** with the number of items
- The badge updates instantly as you add or remove items
- Badge only shows when you have items (no badge = empty cart)

### 3️⃣ View Your Cart
- Click the **Shopping Cart Icon** in the header
- A drawer slides out from the right side
- See all your selected plans with:
  - Plan name
  - Plan description  
  - Plan price
  - **Remove option (×)** if you change your mind

### 4️⃣ Complete Your Purchase
- **Timer:** You have **10 minutes** to complete your purchase
- The countdown shows **MM:SS** format (e.g., 09:45)
- Click **"Continue to payment"** to proceed to checkout
- If the timer expires, your cart is automatically cleared

### 5️⃣ Remove Items (If Needed)
- Click the **×** button on any item to remove it
- The item is removed immediately
- Cart count badge updates in real-time
- You can always add items back later

## Step-by-Step Example

### Scenario: Purchasing Your First Membership

**Step 1: Browse Plans**
- Open the Plans page
- See several membership options (Basic, Premium, Elite, etc.)
- Each shows a price and details

**Step 2: Add to Cart**
- Find the plan you like: "Premium Membership - $79.99/month"
- Click the **"Add to Cart"** button
- Notice the **cart icon badge now shows "1"**

**Step 3: Keep Shopping (Optional)**
- You can continue browsing other plans

- **Cart badge updates to "2"**

**Step 4: Open Your Cart**
- Click the **Shopping Cart Icon** (top-right)
- A drawer slides open from the right

  - **Premium Membership** - $79.99/month [×]

**Step 6: Checkout**
- Click **"Continue to payment"**
- Proceeds to subscription form (same as before)
- Select your payment method
- Complete your purchase

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Can I add the same plan multiple times?
**A:** Yes! Each addition is tracked separately, allowing you to purchase multiple memberships.

### Q: What if I refresh the page?
**A:** Your cart will be cleared. We recommend completing your purchase before refreshing.
*(Future update: Cart will be saved automatically)*

### Q: What happens when the timer expires?
**A:** Your cart is automatically cleared after 10 minutes. You can add items again anytime.

### Q: Can I save my cart for later?
**A:** Currently, your cart is only saved for your current session. We're working on "Save for Later" feature.

### Q: Is my payment info secure?
**A:** Yes! Payment is processed through our secure payment gateway (same as before). Your card information is encrypted.

### Q: Can I use promo codes?
**A:** Yes! During checkout, you can apply any active promotional codes to your purchase.

### Q: What if I add a plan and then navigate away?
**A:** Your cart persists while you're browsing. Your items stay there until you checkout or the timer expires.

### Q: Can I see my cart on mobile?
**A:** Absolutely! The cart works perfectly on phones, tablets, and computers.
